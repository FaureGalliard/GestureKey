## GestureKey 3.2.0 beta 1

**Beta:** gestures now depend on *how you open your hand*, not on how fast you move it.
The mouse is still being tuned.

### Install

Download `GestureKey_3.2.0-beta.1_x64-setup.exe` and run it. It installs for your user
(no administrator needed) and lives in the system tray. It updates earlier betas in
place and keeps your settings.

> Windows SmartScreen may warn you because the installer is not signed yet:
> **More info → Run anyway**.

### What's new

**One idea for windows and the cursor: how you open your hand**
- Holding a window (✊): open your hand **tilted towards a side** to send it to that
  monitor, **upright** to let go, **fingers pointing down** to close it.
- Pointing (☝️): open your hand **tilted towards a side** and the cursor jumps to that
  monitor; upright just lets go of the cursor.
- The fast flicks to a side and downwards are gone: they were confused with each other
  and with quick drags. The flick up to maximize stays.

**Mouse**
- Reach the corners: park your hand at the edge of the camera's view and the cursor
  keeps gliding that way.

**🖼️ New screenshot gesture**
- Frame the screen with both hands in an L (thumb and index stretched), like taking a
  photo, and hold for half a second. Replaces pushing the palm away, which often failed.

**Project**
- GestureKey is now MIT licensed.

### Known issues

- The mouse can still feel imprecise; thresholds will keep changing between betas.
- Opening the hand half-turned (around 90°) sends a window to the side instead of closing it.
- In low light the camera drops its frame rate and recognition gets worse; the app warns you.
