## GestureKey 3.1.0 beta 1

**Beta:** the index-finger mouse is new and its thresholds are still being tuned.

### Install

Download `GestureKey_3.1.0-beta.1_x64-setup.exe` and run it. It installs for your user
(no administrator needed) and lives in the system tray. If you have 3.0.0 beta, it
updates in place and keeps your settings.

> Windows SmartScreen may warn you because the installer is not signed yet:
> **More info → Run anyway**.

### What's new

**☝️ Your index finger is the mouse**
- Point with your index and the cursor follows your hand, across every monitor.
- 🤏 Touch the tips of your thumb and index, then let go: click (twice quickly = double
  click). Touch and move: drag. Hold them together: right click.
- A quick flick of the index to a side takes the cursor to the monitor on that side.
- **Scroll anywhere:** point at the text on any screen and switch to ✌️; whatever is
  under the cursor scrolls, no click needed.

**Task view you can leave**
- ✋✋ bring the palms together to open it; inside, ☝️ point at a window and 🤏 to open it.
- ✋✋ move the palms apart to leave: it opens the window you pointed at, or goes back
  to where you were.

**Windows**
- Moving windows is smooth (120 Hz) and accelerated; a flick to a side sends the window
  to the other monitor, a flick up maximizes it.
- Opening your hand tilted towards a side sends the window to that monitor and maximizes it.
- Hand at the edge of the camera: the window moves on to the next monitor.
- **Close:** throw it down, or drag it to the bottom edge and let go (the notice says
  "Let go to close").
- Windows-style previews of what letting go will do (maximize / close).

**Recognition**
- The engine moved to MediaPipe Tasks: faster (≈23 ms per frame instead of 40 ms).
- The pose model was retrained on a new session plus the v2 dataset (≈94 % on unseen
  data), and now knows ☝️.
- After a gesture ends there is a short pause, so lowering your hand doesn't trigger another one.

**App**
- The interface is available in English, Spanish, French and Korean.

### Known issues

- Mute by gesture is still off; lowering the volume all the way with 🤟 mutes.
- A fist is sometimes read as ☝️; the mouse and window moving make up for it using the
  finger positions, but it can fail.
- In low light the camera drops its frame rate and recognition gets worse; the app warns you.
